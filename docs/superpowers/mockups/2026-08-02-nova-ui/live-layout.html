1 · Prompter deck

The answer stream owns the screen; the conversation is one thin "them · now" ticker. Newest answer sits at the bottom, nearest your thumb and your eye-line. History scrolls up and away. Closest to what exists today, refined.

2 · Split cockpit

Conversation and copilot get permanent halves, divided by a HUD rail. You always see who said what — reassuring, information-dense, and the most "mission control." The cost: the answer you read aloud gets half the room, so smaller type.

3 · Stage

A true teleprompter: the current answer takes center stage in large type — biggest, fastest read of the three. History shrinks to a one-line "previous" chip; the transcript hides behind a pull-down. Maximum glanceability, minimum context.
